# Lists

Lists help you gather items for crafting projects and plan market-board shopping.
Use **Build** to edit what you need and **Shop** to choose a shopping trip.

## Creating a list

1. Open **Lists** (/list) and choose **New list**.
2. Give the list a name.
3. Choose an online list when signed in, or save it **On this device**.

Device lists work without an account and stay in this browser. Use the list's
backup controls to export a copy before clearing browser data. Choose **Make
online** to connect a device list to your account and use it on other devices.

## Building a list

Search for items directly in the list and add the quantity and quality you need.
Switch the composer to **Recipes** to preview finished items or ingredients.
You can also use **Add to List** from an item page or other tools.

Edit **Needed** and **Quality** in each row. The row's check button marks the
whole row acquired (or back to unacquired) in one click; open **Details** to
set an exact **Owned** amount or a target price. The bell button creates a
price alert for that row's item without leaving the list. Items that cannot
be sold on the market board are labelled **Unavailable on market board**
instead of looking merely out of stock.

Select rows for bulk changes. **Select all shown** selects the rows the
current filter displays, and **Delete selected** asks for a second click to
confirm; **Undo** is still offered right after a removal, and **Undo**/**Redo**
correct other edits. Sorting by **Est. cost** orders rows by their full
remaining line cost (the number in the column), and **Qty** sorts by
**Needed**.

The Build estimate prices only the units you still need — not whole
market-board stacks — in your chosen world, datacenter, or region, so its
total can be lower than the old whole-stack estimate; **Shop** below still
plans whole stacks. It calls out incomplete price coverage, and **Cost by
datacenter and world** breaks the total down by where the priced units are
listed.

Use **Buy from** to limit prices to your home world or datacenter, and open
**Exclude** next to it to leave out individual worlds or whole datacenters.
Exclusions apply to both the Build estimate and Shop trips, travel with the
list's link, and can be removed chip by chip or with **Clear**.

## Shopping

Switch to **Shop** to compare trips that buy whole market-board stacks. Choose
your travel limit and route, track purchases, and optionally open the compact
shopping companion. Whole stacks may leave surplus, so the trip total can
differ from the Build estimate.

## Managing and sharing

Rename a list at the top of its editor, or edit its card in the Lists directory.
Online list owners can use **Access** to share with friends or groups and create
invite links. Viewers can browse and plan; editors can change the shared list.
The **More options** menu includes backups for device lists and deletion or
leaving a shared online list.
